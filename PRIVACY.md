# Privacy Policy

**Flags: Widgets & Faces**
Last updated 26 September 2026

## The short version

The app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server to send it to.

## What the app stores

Two things, both on your device:

- **Your favourites** — the flags you have starred.
- **Recently opened flags** — which flags you last looked at, so the widget
  gallery can offer them without you having to star them first.

Both live in the app's own storage, shared between the app and its widgets so
the widgets can show what you picked.

## iCloud

Your favourites are also written to Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so they
follow you between your iPhone and your Apple Watch. That is your iCloud
account, not ours. We cannot read it, and it is covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Turn off iCloud
for the app and everything still works, just per-device.

Recently opened flags are not synced. They stay on the device they happened on.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
- No tracking, and nothing shared with anyone for any purpose.
- No network requests. The flags ship inside the app; there is nothing to
  fetch and nowhere to phone home to.
- No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. The app
  never asks for a permission because it never needs one.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing, so there is nothing it could collect
from a child either.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app and the on-device data goes with it. To clear the iCloud copy,
turn off iCloud Drive for Flags in Settings, or delete the app on every device
signed into that account.

## Changes

If this ever changes, the new version will be in this file with a new date,
and the change will be in the repository's history where anyone can read it.
